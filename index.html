About AS Assistants

SD. SHARU

Founder & Head Assistant

2014 First Shoot · Gudur
2014

The Beginning

AS Assistants was founded and made its first shoot in Gudur, Andhra Pradesh. That same year marked a historic milestone — the first meeting with Hero Bhanuchandra Sir and a visit to his home in Chennai.

2014–2016

Training & Collaboration

Collaborated and worked closely with Hero Bhanuchandra Sir, building industry relationships and honing craft over two formative years of active production experience.

2019–2023

4 Years of Mastery + Awards

Four intensive years of training as an assistant led to recognition at the highest level — earning a Personal Award, Assistant Award, and the prestigious title of Best Assistant.

2023–2025

Best Assistant & Collaborations

Continued receiving the Best Assistant Award. Collaborated with leading Telugu television channels including Gemini TV, ZEE TV, Maa TV, and ETV across iconic serials and major award productions.
